## Full description

TUKI — Little Adventures is a children's coloring experience inspired by the
nature of Costa Rica.

Explore 60 coloring pages arranged in four detail levels: Easy, Medium,
Detailed, and Advanced. Use the brush, fill tool, and eraser; zoom in to work on
small areas, and use Undo or Redo whenever you need them.

Drawings are stored locally on the device so they can be continued from the
gallery. An adult can also export a drawing through Android's secure system
picker.

Verified features:

- 60 coloring pages.
- Four detail levels.
- Brush, fill tool, and eraser.
- Undo and Redo.
- Zoom, save, and restore.
- Drawing gallery.
- Spanish and English interface.
- Offline operation.
- No ads, purchases, accounts, analytics, or tracking.
- Adult gate before exporting a drawing.

TUKI is designed as a calm, family-appropriate creative activity. Drawings and
preferences remain on the device unless an adult chooses to export an image to
a destination of their choice.

## Educational and family context
